Photography in the room

Two color landscape collections: mountain lakes, canyons and glaciers in one frame; waterfalls, woodland streams and forests in the other. The 32 images were selected from Wikimedia Commons Featured Pictures.

These are curated photographs by the credited photographers, not personal travel photographs by Yanbin Xu. Each file retains its individual license. The only changes made here are proportional resizing to a maximum of 768 pixels and JPEG encoding; photographs are not cropped, recolored or generated. The resized CC BY-SA photographs are distributed under the same CC BY-SA version as their source.

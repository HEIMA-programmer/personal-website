# Institution emblems

The Experience cards use genuine university emblems to identify the owner's education institutions. The original PNGs are included unchanged, with a white background and spacing applied by the page; their shapes and colors are not redrawn, cropped, recolored or distorted.

Both 800 × 800 PNGs come from the [RoboMaster university logo collection](https://github.com/RoboMaster/university_logos/tree/3d645fc89869707bd7df8dba0f7e4bbcc4a60e14). Its notice states that logo copyright belongs to the corresponding university. Inclusion in this repository does not place these marks under the website's code license or imply university endorsement.

The marks were visually checked against the design descriptions on the universities' official identity pages: CSU's intertwined C/S/U symbol with the circular bilingual university name, and ZJU's circular Qiushi eagle emblem with the founding year 1897. ZJU's university emblem identifies the parent university of ZJE; it is not presented as a separate ZJE institute emblem.

- [CSU official emblem and usage guidance](https://www.csu.edu.cn/info/1417/8070.htm)
- [ZJU official logo and identity guidance](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/2023/0323/c74903a2732011/page.htm)
- [ZJU official university symbols](https://www.zju.edu.cn/xg/)
